Yarn Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Yarn Sort ("the app") is made by Jay Gosalia, Mumbai, India.

What the app collects. The app does not ask for your name, email, or any account. It does not collect your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Analytics. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how levels are played (for example, which level was completed and how long it took). This data is anonymous and aggregated; it includes a random installation identifier and basic device information (device model, OS version, country, language). It is used only to improve the game. Firebase's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising. The app currently shows no advertising and contains no third-party ad SDKs.

Purchases. The app currently offers no in-app purchases.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
