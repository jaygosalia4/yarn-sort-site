Little Haberdashery — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Little Haberdashery ("the app") is made by Jay Gosalia, Mumbai, India.

What the app collects. The app does not ask for your name, email, or any account. It does not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone.

Analytics. The app uses PostHog (PostHog Inc., servers in the United States) to understand how levels are played (for example, which level was completed and how long it took). This data is anonymous: it includes a random installation identifier created by the app (not tied to you or your device's advertising identity), basic device information (device model, OS version, language) and an approximate country or region derived from the network connection. It is used only to improve the game and is never sold or shared for advertising.

Feedback you choose to send. If you use "Send feedback" in the app's settings, the kind you pick, the text you write and a short technical line (app version, build, platform, operating-system version, the level you have reached, and your device language) are sent to a private Google Form owned by the developer, or, if that fails, to an email you send yourself. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Please do not include personal details you do not want us to read. Feedback is used only to fix problems and improve the game, and is deleted on request.

Go ad-free. You can make a one-time purchase that removes the videos. It is paid through the App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment, and processed by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) so the app can unlock and restore it. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase token or receipt and an anonymous app user id created by the app. Your name and card details never reach the developer.

Platforms. The app is available on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). On Android, the app does not collect the Advertising ID.

Advertising. The app can show a short rewarded video through Google AdMob (Google LLC), but only when you choose to watch one for an extra hint or undo. Videos are never forced and never interrupt play. When a video can be offered, AdMob may collect device identifiers (on Android, the app set identifier, and the Advertising ID is not collected; on iPhone, the app never asks to track you, so the advertising identifier is not available to it), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads (for example, that a video was shown and finished). Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud; see Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you are in the EU, the UK or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy choices" in Settings.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
