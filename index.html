Little Haberdashery

Little Haberdashery is a cosy felt puzzle: unpin buttons from a felt picture, wind the colours each spool asks for, and stitch a picture in the hoop.

Questions, bugs, or a level that feels wrong: email jaygosalia4@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and the level number.

Your progress is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no forced ads, and no in-app purchases in this release.
